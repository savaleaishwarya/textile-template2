Frequently Asked Questions

Have Questions? We Have Answers.

What is the minimum bulk order quantity?

Our minimum bulk order quantity typically starts from 50 pieces, but it may vary depending on the product and customization requirements. Contact us for a detailed quote.

Do you provide custom logo embroidery or printing?

Yes. We offer logo embroidery and printing on shirts, coats, aprons, caps and more. Share your artwork and we will send a sample for approval.

What fabrics do you use for uniforms?

We work with cotton, poly-cotton blends, twill and drill fabrics. Each is chosen for comfort, durability and easy care in its industry.

What is the delivery timeline for bulk orders?

Most bulk orders ship within 10–21 working days after design approval, depending on quantity and branding work.

Do you supply uniforms outside Lucknow?

Yes. We deliver across India through trusted logistics partners and track every shipment until it arrives.
